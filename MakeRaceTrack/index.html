One idea, three moves

Imagine it. Assemble it. Race it.

The track is both your strategy and your playground, so every placement changes what happens next.

  1. Read the field

    Find the start, the finish and the useful layers, then picture a route before committing the first tile.

  2. Shape an advantage

    Use bends, straights, ramps and boost lanes to make a fast path while limiting unnecessary distance.

  3. Watch the result

    Complete the route, race the rival and compare the finish, maximum speed and final distance.
