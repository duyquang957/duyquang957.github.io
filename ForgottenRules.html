Full description

Play it your way

As an open-world game, Forgotten Rules lets players freely choose what they want to do. Play in first person or third person and move naturally between driving, action and sightseeing.

Race through a large city, compete with street racers, start with a simple car and earn more valuable vehicles. Customize a variety of models—or try the surprise of racing a bus.

Plan a bank robbery, find a route inside, react to difficult situations and escape the police. Customize weapons when the situation demands it. Coins are also added while you play, so you can steadily equip more advanced items without following a fixed quest line.
